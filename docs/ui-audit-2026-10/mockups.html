Fieldwatch Borrow Book

An audit of how Supercell, Riot and the best mobile roguelites lay out their screens and handle choices, items and placement. It ends with 20 mockups of what Fieldwatch could borrow so that each battle, build and march round needs fewer words and fewer taps.

Part 1 · Where we are

The current loop, screen by screen

Captured from a real run of today's build. The four-band Root Shell already does a lot right: the field is never covered, there is one CTA and the art direction holds. The friction comes from prose standing in for feedback, every option being shown at once and controls packed into small columns.

  1. Coach text instead of coaching. 13 one-time tips, shown one at a time, such as "Items you win land in your pack. Tap a slot under Gear…". Each takes 60–90px and pushes the field down while it's up.
  2. Placement has no guidance. Every legal tile glows the same. The range ring and the value of a tile only show after you commit.
  3. The Selector band is mostly empty. With one or two heroes, a 126px band holds a single 92px card.
  4. The header spends a row on the wordmark. "FIELDWATCH" and a full-width cargo bar take 76px on every screen.
  5. Enemy intent is hidden. The wave is one icon with "×8" and a "Details" button. Armour, speed and sub-wave structure are invisible.
  6. Gear and skill effects can't be seen. Nothing on the field shows a skill or affix firing, so "+9% crit" is something you have to trust.
  7. Loot asks you to compare in your head. There's no "vs equipped" delta, no slot-fit hint and no fast equip. Pack, doll and detail share 360px.
  8. The economy is explained in prose. Purse vs bank, road tax, interest capped at 40 and city pay are taught in text strings, never in a receipt.
  9. Threat is an abstract percentage. "+70%", "+380%" and "+530%" next to nodes can't be compared at a glance.
  10. Rewards land with no ceremony. Wave clear is a static ribbon. Gold, XP and level-ups don't move into the counters they fill.
Part 2 · What the best do

Three schools of mobile game UI

The full write-up with sources is in AUDIT.md next to this page. These are the patterns that matter for a portrait autobattler.

Supercell

Clash Royale · Clash of Clans · Brawl Stars · Boom Beach · Hay Day · Squad Busters · mo.co · Clash Mini · CR Merge Tactics
  • Bottom half is the thumb zone. Every control in CR lives in the bottom band; the arena is the top 75%.
  • Battle is the centre tab and the default. In 2025 a mode switcher was placed right beside it because buried modes went unplayed.
  • Drag or tap-tap to deploy, with no confirm. A ghost preview, a red no-deploy zone, snap to grid. Placing is instant.
  • Hand plus "Next" card. Players plan one step ahead without opening a screen.
  • Strict colour roles. Yellow is the main action, green is upgrade, red is a badge or something you can't afford, blue is chrome.
  • Rewards fly into counters. Coins arc into the total and the number counts up. Tap-to-reveal drops are decided before the animation plays.
  • Features unlock by milestone. One popup per session at most; everything else is a badge.
  • One nudge during an autobattle (Boom Beach flare). The player gets a single meaningful input, not micromanagement.

Riot Games

Teamfight Tactics (+ mobile) · Legends of Runeterra / Path of Champions · League · Wild Rift · Valorant
  • Put a cause next to its effect. TFT moved Buy XP, Level and Odds side by side because players never connected them.
  • "Tough choices, not touch execution." Auto star-up and auto-combine remove busywork.
  • Preview before commit. Item recipes appear while dragging. LoR's Oracle's Eye shows the outcome. PoC shop items are shown already attached.
  • Trait tracker. "2/4" breakpoints with tier colours, live as units move.
  • Pick 1 of 3 with a reroll per card and a hide toggle to look at the board while you decide.
  • Owned-unit glow in the shop. Your roster's matches stand out without you having to remember them.
  • Ask for the gold, show the maths. Hovering gold shows next round's income. Valorant shows a "min next round" figure.
  • Visual impact should match gameplay impact (League VFX style guide), with brightness contrast as the main tool.

Mobile roguelites

Balatro · Slay the Spire · Kingdom Rush · Bloons TD 6 · Super Auto Pets · Hades · Vampire Survivors · Archero · Backpack Battles · Loop Hero · Ball x Pit · Thronefall
  • Scoring is a performance. Balatro explains every number by playing it: chips, then mult, with each joker wiggling as it fires.
  • A ring around the tower (Kingdom Rush). Tap a plot and options with prices circle your thumb. Tap outside to cancel.
  • Panel opposite the selection (BTD6), with three upgrade tracks shown as pips and a one-tap targeting toggle.
  • Intents (StS). Show what is coming before you commit.
  • Rarity frame + slot icon + NEW/UPGRADE badge (Hades). Fit and power read before any text.
  • Freeze, drag to merge, fixed shop layout (SAP, Balatro). The shop reads the same every time.
  • Skip always pays something (StS bowl, Balatro tags, VS XP).
  • Don't gate core info, and keep destructive drop zones away from reorder drags (Balatro's sell-zone patches).
The five rules the mockups follow
Show it, don't caption itReplace every coach sentence with a ghost, a glow, a delta or a receipt.
One gesture per verbDrag to place, equip and merge. Tap to select. Hold to inspect. No select-then-confirm unless it spends.
Preview before commitRange, coverage, stat deltas and merge results are visible while the finger is still down.
Field on top, thumbs at the bottomThe stage gets 65–85% of the height. Every control sits in the bottom band.
Rewards travelGold, XP and loot visibly move into the counter, bar or slot they change.
