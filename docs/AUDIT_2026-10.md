# Fieldwatch: holistic audit and improvement plan (October 2026)

Branch `claude/compassionate-johnson-pzt0qc` off `main @ 1b43846`. This follows the UI benchmark in
`docs/ui-audit-2026-10/` (Supercell, Riot and mobile roguelites, plus 20 mockups).

## How this audit was done

Four audits ran in parallel against the real build. Each one played the game, measured it, or both.

| Lane | Method |
| --- | --- |
| **Loop and feel** | Two full runs on a 390×844 touch phone with Playwright: a first-run escort, and a veteran 1-crate contract through a city's cash-out. Timed waves, counted taps, read the FX counters, forced a leak. |
| **Economy and progression** | Read the spec (`MERCENARY_COMPANY.md`), `balance/REPORT.md` and every rule in `src/game/run/`. Two scratch simulations call the real rule functions: the cash-out EV at every stake × city, and unlock pacing over 200 saves. |
| **UI conformance (Whales)** | An automated scan of 36 screens (phone and desk) against the Whales house rules, which come from the recorded critiques in `docs/whales-critique/`. Every screenshot was also read by eye. |
| **Technical health** | Typecheck, tests, build, bundle, CI history, frame times on a 4×-throttled phone, cold load on Fast 3G, offline boot, accessibility basics. |

**About Whales.** The Whales MCP server was not reachable from this session: it isn't an installed connector and none of its tools are loaded. Its judgement is applied through what it recorded in this repo. That means the 11 screen critiques in `docs/whales-critique/critiques.json`, the approved plan in `PLAN.md`, and the conformance notes in the review log. The house rules derived from them:

1. Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, or ≥ 3:1 for large text; aim for about 7:1 on small text.
2. Text ≥ 11px.
3. Icons that carry meaning on their own are ≥ 36 CSS px (72 image px at 2×). An icon beside a label is exempt.
4. The next step is the loudest thing on screen, and there is one primary treatment.
5. Reading order ends on the primary.
6. Chrome takes no more than about 30% of the first screen.
7. Titles outrank their body text.
8. Spacing sits on the 4/8/10/12/16/20/24/32 scale.
9. The selected option dominates; the alternatives stay equal weight.
10. Dark art stays legible.
11. Touch targets are ≥ 44px.
12. The Root Shell rules hold, especially "the Stage is sacred".

When Whales is reachable again, re-critique the screens this plan changes. The scan in §5 is a stand-in, not a replacement.

## 1. What the game is for

From `MERCENARY_COMPANY.md` and the shipped code: **you run a mercenary militia hired to get a trade caravan down a company's road.**

- **The Gate is the caravan.** Cargo % is its health, and every leak costs money at the next city.
- **The headline decision is "cash out or press on"** at each city: push your luck.
- **The long game is standing with five companies**, an HQ, sealed crates, and the Sovereign Route as the endgame.
- The game is mobile-first and portrait: one hand, short sessions.

Every finding below is judged against that: does it make the caravan, the money and the push-your-luck moment matter, and is it effortless on a phone?

## 2. What is already good

- **First contact is fast.** First action at 2.5 s, first wave at 9.5 s.
- **The Root Shell holds.** The field is never covered by a sheet.
- **The juice exists.** In one wave the FX counters recorded 25 floaters, 29 hitstops, 39 shake events and 17 kills. A leak has its own sound, music duck, floater and barricade flash, and wave clear has a count-up ceremony.
- **Placement has a press-and-slide preview,** and there is a keyboard and screen-reader path through the tiles.
- **Reduced motion is honoured** end to end. There is a global focus ring and one polite announcer.
- **Engineering health:** 714 tests pass, typecheck and build are green, the battle sim is deterministic, and offline boot works.
- **The menu, the contract terms and the city screen** carry the trade-route fantasy strongly.

## 3. Findings, ranked by how much they hurt the intent

### A. The push-your-luck pillar has nothing at stake (critical)

Both the loop audit and the economy audit found this independently.

- **Cash out pays 0 gold more often than not.** It is 0 for an escort at every city, 0 for a 1-crate stake, and 0 at the second city for anything under 4 crates (`contracts.cashOutValue`, `CASH_OUT_RATE` 0.5).
- **A fall costs almost nothing.** It keeps every city's pay, the leftover purse and 25% of road gold (`settle.runDeposit`).
- **So pressing on wins at all 18 stake × city decision points,** and the policy moves contract pay by −1 to +7 gold (REPORT §13).
- **The first contract has no cash-out at all** (`contractSlice.cashOut`, the `firstRun` guard), so the headline fantasy is missing from the run that decides whether a player stays.
- **`cratesSoldAt` leaves no crate for the destination** on small stakes, so contract pay is not monotone in the stake (the dips at 4 and 8 crates).

### B. Fights don't warn before a leak, and the holds cost dead taps (high)

- **A single leak can take 55% of the cargo with no warning.** A boss walked past an archer whose range missed the final straight, and cargo went from 100% to 45%. All the feedback arrives after the hit, and the wagon sprite always shows three crates.
- **Every sub-wave hold needs a tap on "Next".** With one or two heroes the "move one hero" option is usually pointless, so that is about 30 dead taps a run.
- **The coach tip band appears mid-wave** and pushes the Stage down about 45 px at the moment of a leak.

### C. The UI breaks Whales' rules in the same places Whales flagged before (high)

- **Wave cleared:** "Take it" is smaller than the reward cards, and the wave strip renders *below* it. This is Whales' round-1 and round-2 finding, still open.
- **Rarity colours used as text fail AA:** Epic 4.08–4.38:1, Mythic 3.09–3.87:1.
- **Settings:** the toggle "Mute" takes the page's only primary CTA.
- **Desk holds:** "Next ▶" is 87×44 in a 455px rail.
- **Chrome:** 32.9% of the first screen on the merchant and 40.4% on run lost. The page fade dims real rows, and there are 250–360 px gaps on the shrine, campfire and city pages.
- **Text under 11px:** 8px on the strip's rarity letter, 9.35px on the header's "/12".
- **Run map:** the route panel clips the reachable row.
- **Shrine:** the curse reads weaker than the boon.
- **City receipt:** dim brown lines on brown.

### D. Difficulty and economy shape (medium; needs tuning)

- **Act 3 is the whole run.** Survival goes 79% at depth 8, then 65 / 39 / 25 / 16%. A zero-meta escort delivers 14–18%, and the first-timer gate fails (14% against a 15% floor).
- **The bank runs on road gold, not the contract.** Contract pay is 21% of an escort's net.
- **Dead or never-taken options:**
  - the Finance office pays back in about 260 runs;
  - "Fewer boulders" makes runs 4pt harder;
  - Bounty is worth 0.25 gold a kill;
  - Tithe Box and Field Surgeon measure about 0.
- **The purse picker is a trap.** A purse of 0 is close to always best.
- **The item track is hollow.** Many item kinds share a base (Plate = Mail), and pulls, not play, are how items unlock.
- **The run-2 wall:** the board, stakes, purse, HQ, crates, focus and market all open after any first finished run, a loss included.

### E. Technical (medium)

- **The balance CI gate has been red for weeks.** 50 invariants fail on the committed state, and the job takes 17–24 minutes. It can no longer flag a regression.
- **CI deprecations:** `checkout@v4` and `setup-node@v4` run on Node 20, and `ubuntu-latest` moves on 2026-10-19.
- **One 737 KB JS chunk and an empty `#root`:** nothing paints for 3.3 s on Fast 3G.
- **On a throttled phone a wave runs at 29 fps.** The caravan is re-rasterised with hundreds of `fillRect` calls per frame, and the breather grid re-strokes about 250 rounded rects per frame.
- **Saves:** a failed write is silent, and `navigator.storage.persist()` is never called.
- **Service worker:** navigation is network-first with no timeout, and an open standalone session never checks for updates.

## 4. The plan

Each item is marked by who decides:
- **Ship**: within the spec's own placeholders or the house rules.
- **Default**: a design call made here because the intent clearly needs it. Every one is a single named constant, so the designer can move it.
- **Designer**: written up, not built.

### Lane 1: make the push-your-luck real (economy)

| # | Change | Decides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1 | `CASH_OUT_RATE` 0.5 → 1.0. Cashing out sells what is on the wagons at full value; you give up the completion bonus, the item chances and the contract skill. | Ship |
| 1.2 | `cratesSoldAt`: first city ⌈c/2⌉, destination ⌈rest/2⌉, second city the rest. Pay becomes monotone in the stake and the destination always has cargo to sell. | Ship |
| 1.3 | **A fall forfeits most of the road's gold.** A lost contract banks `LOST_ROAD_SHARE` (10%) of road gold instead of `ROAD_SHARE` (25%). City pay and the leftover purse are still kept, so "never broke" holds. This is the stake in "press on". | Default |
| 1.4 | The city screen states the trade in numbers: cash out banks X now; press on risks Y (road gold and crates) for Z. When there is truly nothing to lose, it says so instead of staging two cards. | Ship |
| 1.5 | The first contract offers cash-out at its **second** city. The first city stays a teaching beat. | Default |
| 1.6 | `ACT_STEPS[2]` 1.29 → 1.23, so act 3 stops being the whole run. This is the cheapest lever in REPORT §11's own fit table. | Ship |

### For the designer (written up, not built)

1. **Re-anchor the balance targets on contract delivery.** For example: zero-meta escort about 25%, veteran escort 45–55%, max stake 15–20%. Retire §6's band, which models a team the contract game doesn't produce.
2. **Purse picker:** remove it in favour of fixed starting gold not taken from the bank, or keep it. Today 0 is close to always best.
3. **HQ:** cut or rebuild Finance (dead), and merge Operations into two or three purchases with a felt effect. "Fewer boulders" measures −4pt.
4. **Item identities:** give each item kind one stat identity, or collapse the duplicates. Pay a bonus for standing levels past the card pool.
5. **Charter:** it is gold-negative against an escort (+80 vs +603). Raise the payout, lower the fee, or let it earn standing.
6. **Stagger the run-2 reveal:** HQ at the first 500 banked, crates at the first delivery, stakes at standing 2, market and focus at run 5.
7. **Fight variety:** one field per run means posts carry over and fights collapse to Start / Next / Take after fight 1. Options: a second entry lane per act, flyers, or a priority target per wave.
8. **One-tap commits for cheap choices** (rewards, campfire). This goes against shell rule 1 (show detail before committing), so it is the designer's call.
9. **Tuning for never-taken options:** Bounty ×3, Tithe Box 25, Iron Vigil −25%, Cleave L1 −30%, Wildfire L2 −15%, Epic stat relics up.

- The Whales-rule scan (`scripts/whales-scan.mjs`) re-run on the same 36 screens, before against after.
