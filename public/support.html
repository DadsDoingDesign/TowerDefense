Contact

Found a bug, lost a save, or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/DadsDoingDesign/TowerDefense/issues. Please include your device, iOS version and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. There are no accounts and no cloud saves, so deleting the app deletes your progress.

There is no sound. Check the ring/silent switch on the side of your iPhone and the volume sliders in the game's Settings. Sound starts after your first tap.

Something on screen is too small. Settings has a larger text size and colour-vision modes.

Does the game collect my data? No. See the privacy policy.
