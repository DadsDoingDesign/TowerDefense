Privacy Policy

Merchant Mercenaries (MerchMercs) · Last updated 24 October 2026

Short version: the game collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends your data to us or to anyone else.

What the game stores

Your progress (gold, unlocks, settings, the run in progress and your militia's name and banner) is saved on your device only. In the iOS app it is kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone; on the web it is kept in your browser's local storage. We cannot see it.

Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes it. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud, Apple may include the app's storage in that backup under Apple's own privacy terms.

What the game sends

Nothing. The iOS app contains everything it needs and makes no network requests while you play. The web version downloads the game's files from its host (Vercel), which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security and operations.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Purchases

If the game ever offers in-app purchases, they are handled by Apple. We receive no payment details. This policy will be updated before anything changes.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: see the support page.
